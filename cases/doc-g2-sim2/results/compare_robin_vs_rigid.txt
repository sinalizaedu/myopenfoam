# G2 Sim2 — Robin vs rigid wall (IVI, on-axis tip, M1)
# Baseline IOP steady ≈ 15.004 mmHg (simpleFoam), d_TM=7.779e14, Q_ivi=2.08325e-9 m³/s (¼ eye)
# Experiments: 2026-07-22 (no p-shift; init from baseline p/U)

| Wall BC | Peak AC [mmHg] | t_peak AC | Peak vitreous [mmHg] | t_peak vit | IOP @ t=20 AC |
|---------|---------------:|----------:|---------------------:|-----------:|--------------:|
| Robin (volume-matched) | 25.00 | 5.40 s | 56.22 | 5.90 s | 14.675 |
| Rigid (noSlip)         | 793.78 | 7.90 s | 830.75 | 7.90 s | 14.675 |

Robin reduces IVI peak IOP by ~97% (AC) and ~93% (vitreous) vs rigid wall.
Recovery to transient repouso by t=20 s in both cases.
